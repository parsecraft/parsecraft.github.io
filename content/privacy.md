## The short version

This blog does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or comment systems, and it does not ask you to create an account. There is very little to say here, on purpose.

## What happens when you visit

This site is a set of static pages served by GitLab Pages. When you load a page, your browser requests files directly from GitLab's infrastructure, which, like any web host, logs standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and requested URL) for operation and security. I do not receive, store, or use that data myself. See [GitLab's privacy statement](https://about.gitlab.com/privacy/) for how they handle it.

- No first-party cookies are set by this site.
- No third-party trackers, ad networks, or social-media pixels are embedded.
- The search page runs entirely in your browser against an index built when the site is generated. Your search terms never leave your device.
- The light/dark theme choice is remembered using your browser's local storage, on your device only.

## If this changes

If I ever add privacy-respecting, cookie-less analytics, I will update this page first.

## Links to other sites

Posts may link to outside sources. Once you click through, that site's own privacy policy applies.
